Modifications
-------------
Each file was converted from stereo 44.1 kHz to mono 22.05 kHz, trimmed to the
opening keystroke (78 ms), faded at the edges, and peak-normalised to -1 dBFS.
Playback variation is applied at runtime and does not alter these files.
